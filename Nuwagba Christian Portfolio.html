01 / About

About

In 2020, when the world moved online overnight, I had to teach myself how to learn again. That's where this started.

The shift to e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic forced me to get hands-on with technology out of necessity. What began as survival turned into fascination — not just with what software does, but with how it actually runs the hardware underneath it. That curiosity turned into a decision: study software engineering properly.

I'm now in my fourth year of a Software Engineering degree at Eastern Mediterranean University, in Famagusta, North Cyprus — a long way from Kampala. Along the way I completed a coding bootcamp run through Makerere University, CISCO and CiPSD-certified, covering programming, networking, and graphics design. I'm still chasing the same question that started it all: how do these systems actually work, and what can I build once I understand them?

Student Life in Northern Cyprus

When I moved from Kampala to Famagusta for university, most of what I knew about visas, accommodation, and everyday costs came from scattered WhatsApp groups and guesswork. So I built the guide I wish I'd had: a site walking incoming Ugandan students through visas, accommodation, transportation, health services, safety, food, and realistic monthly costs.
