01 / About

Curiosity became
a career direction.

My interest in technology became serious in 2020, when learning suddenly moved online and I had to understand the tools I was relying on every day.

That curiosity grew into software engineering: not only wanting to use technology, but wanting to understand what sits underneath it, why systems behave the way they do, and how to build better ones.

I’m now in my fourth year at Eastern Mediterranean University in Famagusta. Alongside university, I’ve built web projects, completed an ICT bootcamp covering programming, networking and graphic design, and kept developing the creative side of my work through visual design.
